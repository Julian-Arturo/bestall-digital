## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion on Vercel. Custom domain: bestalldigital.com (DNS at Banana Host).

## Users

Primary: Spanish-speaking business owners and founders (Colombia / LatAm) who need a marketing + technology partner to get traffic, convert, and ship digital products — arriving cold from social, referral, or direct domain. Secondary: prospects evaluating Bestall via live product demos (affiliate funnels already in market).

Job to be done: understand what Bestall Digital does in seconds, trust capability via real shipped products, and request a conversation / hire.

## Product Purpose

**Bestall Digital** is a marketing & technology agency site. The root domain sells the agency. Two live product landings act as proof of work / experience:

- `/vip` — Mecánica VIP (moto mechanics affiliate funnel)
- `/diabetes` — “Toma el Control: 21 días” (diabetes program affiliate funnel)

Success = clear agency offer + memorable first impression + CTAs to contact + clicks into demo products.

## Positioning

Not a template agency brochure: a studio that **ships conversion landings and growth systems** — proven by real live funnels on the same domain, not mock case studies.

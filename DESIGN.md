# Design System: Bestall Digital

> Scope: agency home `/` only. `/vip` and `/diabetes` keep their own systems.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "La Sala de Cierre"**

A closing-room website: cool paper, ink, teal signal, one coral WhatsApp plate.
Full-bleed hero photo. Services as ruled rows. Live product proof. No enamel
signboards, no purple agency template, no card grids as page structure.

Seed `ff5a5e9e` · grounded index 6 · replaces EL AVISO PINTADO.
